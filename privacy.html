Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Summary

The Daud Trade FX Calculator is a static web page. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything you type into the calculator stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The calculator runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your inputs (starting capital, targets, timeframes) are processed locally on your device and are discarded when you leave or refresh the page. We do not operate a server, database, or user accounts for this tool.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies and we do not run any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this site.
